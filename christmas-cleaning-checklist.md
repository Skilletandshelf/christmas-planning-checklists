# Christmas Cleaning Checklist for a Small Apartment
A simple room-by-room reset before Christmas — 15 minutes at a time, no deep-clean marathon needed.

## Kitchen
- [ ] Clear and wipe down the counters
- [ ] Clean the stovetop and microwave inside and out
- [ ] Empty and wipe the fridge, and toss anything expired
- [ ] Wash the dish towels and put out a fresh set
- [ ] Take out the trash and recycling, and line the bins with fresh bags

## Living room
- [ ] Pick up clutter and put everything back in its place
- [ ] Dust shelves, the TV stand, and light fixtures
- [ ] Vacuum or sweep the floor, including under the sofa cushions
- [ ] Fluff the cushions and fold a clean throw blanket over the sofa
- [ ] Make space for the tree and decorations before putting them up

## Bathroom
- [ ] Scrub the sink, toilet, and shower or tub
- [ ] Wipe the mirror and faucet until they shine
- [ ] Put out fresh hand towels and a full roll of toilet paper
- [ ] Empty the trash and restock soap and guest essentials

## Entryway
- [ ] Shake out the doormat and sweep the entry floor
- [ ] Clear the coat hooks and shoe area so guests have space
- [ ] Wipe the front door handle and light switch
- [ ] Hang a small wreath or a simple festive touch by the door
